Train on Synthetic Data

No data? Generate a synthetic labeled dataset that combines the feature schemas of fake-profile-detector and spam_identifier, then train the model.

The synthetic generator produces realistic behavioral, content, network, and image features plus spam_identifier's sentiment, country, account-type, word-frequency, and link features, with a threat label derived from known threat patterns.

What happens during training
  1. Load and validate the labeled dataset (target column is_threat).
  2. Encode categorical features and scale numeric features.
  3. Train and compare 8 ML algorithms (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Logistic Regression, Neural Network, SVM, Naive Bayes, KNN, Decision Tree).
  4. Select the best model by F1 score and AUC.
  5. Persist the model, scaler, label encoders, and feature names to models/.
